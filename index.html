04 · Research

Research
Spotlight

Graduate research at Arizona State University, Jan 2025 – Apr 2026 — where process engineering meets materials science.

Arizona State University

Process–Structure–Property in LPBF Metal Composites

Characterised how powder feedstock parameters in a laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) metal matrix composite — ball-milled 316L + TiC — propagate through microstructure to mechanical performance, and where failures actually begin.

  • Root-cause failure analysis combining in-situ neutron diffraction with FE simulation — coarse-particle interfaces identified as preferential crack-initiation sites.
  • 5 ABAQUS/Explicit models built and validated with Johnson-Cook plasticity/damage criteria to predict yield, hardening and failure.
  • Mesh-sensitivity study (0.35–1.4 µm elements) establishing convergence and repeatability before drawing engineering conclusions.
  • Mechanical response quantified across 5 microstructural configurations, translated into design and process guidelines for reinforcement distribution.
  • Neutron diffraction data from Oak Ridge National Laboratory cross-validated against modelling — thesis approved by a 3-member faculty committee.
